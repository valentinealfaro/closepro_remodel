## 💡 CEO Decision Points

### Decision 1: Pricing Aggressiveness (Day 7)
- **Conservative:** Keep at $99/$229/$499 (maximize conversions)
- **Moderate:** Add annual discount 20% off (balance revenue/growth)
- **Aggressive:** Increase to $129/$259/$599 (premium positioning)

**Recommendation:** Stay at current pricing for first 30 days (maximize signups), then test increases if conversion strong

### Decision 2: Paid Ads Trigger (Day 14)
- **If signups < 10/day:** Start paid ads immediately
- **If signups 10-20/day:** Hold until Day 30, then evaluate
- **If signups > 20/day:** Focus on organic only, scale ads slowly

**Recommendation:** Launch with $200-300/month in Google Ads on Day 14 regardless; scale based on ROAS

### Decision 3: Outbound Sales (Day 21)
- **If conversions < 25%:** Have CEO make 10 cold calls to close manually
- **If conversions 25-35%:** Light outbound to high-value targets
- **If conversions > 35%:** Keep outbound light, focus on organic + paid

**Recommendation:** Schedule first 10 demo calls by Day 7; measure conversion
